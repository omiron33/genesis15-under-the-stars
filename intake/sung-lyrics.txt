The Lord’s word came to Abram in a vision:
“Do not fear, Abram. I am your shield;
your reward will be very great.”
Abram said, “Master, Lord,
what will you give me?
I depart without a child.
This Eliezer of Damascus,
son of Masek, my home-born woman servant,
is my heir.”
Abram said, “I am grieved:
you have given me no seed.
My home-born servant
will inherit from me.”
At once the Lord’s voice came:
“He will not be your heir.
One born from you
will be your heir.”
He brought Abram outside:
“Look up at heaven; count the stars,
if you can number them all.”
Then he said, “So shall your children be.”
Abram believed God,
and God counted it to him as righteousness.
He said, “I am God, who brought you
from the land of the Chaldeans
to give you this land as your inheritance.”
Abram said, “Master, Lord,
how can I know I will inherit it?”
He said, “Bring me a heifer
in her third year,
a she-goat in her third year,
and a ram in his third year,
a dove and a pigeon.”
Abram brought them, cut the animals in two,
and laid each half opposite its mate.
But he did not divide the birds.
Birds came down upon the bodies,
upon the divided pieces;
Abram sat down beside them.
At sunset a trance fell upon Abram;
a great and gloomy terror fell on him.
The Lord said to Abram,
“Know for certain:
your seed will be strangers
in a land not their own.
They will be enslaved, afflicted and humbled
for four hundred years.”
“I will judge the nation they serve.
Afterward they will come forth here
with great possessions.”
“You will go to your fathers in peace,
nourished in a good old age.”
“In the fourth generation they will return here,
for the Amorites’ sins are not yet complete.”
As the sun went down, a flame appeared:
a smoking furnace and fiery lamps
passed between the divided pieces.
That day the Lord made a covenant with Abram:
“To your descendants I give this land,
from Egypt’s river to the great Euphrates.”
“The Kenites, the Kenezites,
and the Kedmoneans,”
the Chettites, the Pherezites,
and the Raphaim;
the Amorites, the Chananites,
the Evites, the Gergesites,
and the Jebusites.
